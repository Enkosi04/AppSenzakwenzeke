Our Story

Based in the heart of Bothas Hill, Durban, Senza Kwenzeke Decor and Catering was founded with one mission: to turn your dream event into reality. From intimate lobola celebrations to grand weddings, we bring authentic Zulu tradition mixed with modern elegance.

What started as a small family business has grown into Durban's trusted name for decor and catering. We don't just decorate - we create memories that last forever.

Our Mission

To deliver affordable, beautiful and stress-free events for every family in KZN.

Our Vision

To be the #1 decor & catering brand in KwaZulu-Natal known for quality and culture.

Our Values

Ubuntu, Respect, Creativity, and Excellence in every event we touch.
